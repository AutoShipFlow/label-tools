# Crop Blank Space from Shipping Label PDF

[简体中文](../../../zh-CN/label-printing/crop-blank-space-from-label-pdf.md)

## Problem

The label content appears inside a large blank PDF page. When printed, the printer scales the whole page, so the useful label area becomes too small.

## Common Causes

- The platform exported the label on an A4 or Letter page.
- The label content has large white margins around it.
- The PDF page size is correct, but the visible label content is much smaller.
- The printer is scaling the full page instead of the label content.

## How to Fix It

Use [Label Printing AI](https://www.autoshipflow.com/tools/label-printing-ai) for print-size problems, or [Crop Label](https://www.autoshipflow.com/tools/crop-label) for direct crop work:

1. Upload the label PDF.
2. Choose the target label paper size.
3. Describe the issue, such as `crop the blank space around the label`.
4. Check that barcode and text are complete in the preview.
5. Print or download the fixed PDF.

## What the Tool Can and Cannot Fix

The tool can remove safe blank space and create a cleaner printable PDF.

It should not blindly crop too close to barcodes, addresses, or carrier marks. Always check the preview before printing a large batch.
