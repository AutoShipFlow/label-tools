# Collecting cases: print multiple small SKU labels on one 4x6 label

We are collecting cases where users have small SKU, product, bin, or warehouse labels, but only have larger 4 x 6 in or 100 x 150 mm label paper available.

中文说明：这个 issue 用来收集“小标签拼到大标签纸上打印”的仓库场景。

## What we are trying to understand

This is a possible N-up layout workflow: placing multiple small labels on one larger label sheet so users can print once and cut them apart.

We want to understand:

- Common small label sizes
- Whether users want 2, 4, 6, 8, or more labels per page
- Whether the source label is PDF, image, CSV, or generated from SKU data
- Whether cut lines, spacing, or margins are needed
- Whether the output paper is 4 x 6 in, 4 x 4 in, A4, or Letter

## Please share

- Source label size
- Target paper size
- Desired number of labels per page
- Whether labels need cut marks or gaps
- Whether each small label is the same design or unique
- Warehouse use case, such as product label, SKU label, bin label, or FNSKU label

## Privacy note

Please do not upload labels with private product, customer, or order information unless it is fully masked.
