# Collecting cases: thermal printer paper size does not match label PDF

We are collecting cases where the PDF label size, browser print settings, printer driver paper size, and actual paper loaded in the thermal printer do not match.

中文说明：这个 issue 用来收集“PDF 尺寸、打印窗口、打印机设置、实际标签纸不一致”的案例。

## What we are trying to understand

Paper mismatch can cause labels to print too small, too large, shifted, cut off, or across two pages.

We want to understand:

- Which paper sizes are most confusing, such as 4 x 6 in vs 100 x 150 mm
- Whether users know the real paper loaded in the printer
- Whether printer presets differ from browser paper size
- Whether the same PDF prints correctly in one tool but not another

## Please share

- PDF page size, if known
- Real label paper size
- Browser paper size selected
- Printer driver paper size selected
- Printer model
- What the browser preview shows
- What the physical print looks like

## Privacy note

Please remove customer names, addresses, phone numbers, tracking numbers, and barcodes before sharing files or screenshots.
