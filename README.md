# Label Tools

Free online tools and practical guides for shipping label PDFs, thermal label printing issues, barcode labels, and ecommerce label workflows.

## What This Project Covers

- Fix labels that print too small, too large, or across two pages
- Resize shipping label PDFs to 4 x 6 in, 100 x 150 mm, 4 x 4 in, and 100 x 100 mm
- Crop blank space from PDF labels before printing
- Convert label images to printable PDFs
- Scan barcodes and QR codes from shipping labels
- Extract tracking numbers from PDF or image labels

## Common Label Printing Problems

If your shipping label prints too small, has too much blank space, prints across two pages, or does not match your thermal label paper, the issue is usually one of these:

- The PDF page size does not match the real label paper.
- The label content is surrounded by large blank margins.
- The PDF has CropBox or Rotate metadata that changes how it appears in viewers.
- The browser print dialog uses the wrong paper size, scale, or margin settings.

AutoShipFlow tools adjust the PDF layout. They cannot read or change the actual paper loaded in your printer, so always check paper size and scale in the print dialog before printing.
